Four distinct visual directions for the Mélange event landing page (30 June 2026). Each thumbnail is a live, working mockup — click Open in full to inspect any of them. The React build will follow whichever direction you choose.

I

Editorial Letterpress

A stately wedding-invitation aesthetic. Ivory, burgundy and brushed gold; high-contrast serif typography; Roman numerals on the milestones; symmetric, centred composition. The safest, most traditional choice.

Type
Cormorant Garamond · Italiana · Manrope
Voice
Restrained · classical · ceremonial
Best for
Guests who expect a formal invitation

II

Jaipur Heirloom

The warmth of an Indian heirloom wedding card, refined and editorial. Cream + Jaipur pink + marigold + emerald; mihrab arch in the hero; block-print dividers; hand-drawn marigold motifs. Festive without being kitschy.

Type
Marcellus · Cormorant italic · Outfit
Voice
Warm · joyful · Indian heritage
Best for
Leaning into the Rajasthani context
Open in full ↗
III

Midnight Brass

A modern black-tie gala. Deep midnight blue with cream type and brushed champagne brass accents; restrained ornament; dramatic negative space; cinematic photography. Wallpaper magazine, not wedding card.

Type
Fraunces (variable) · Outfit · JetBrains Mono
Voice
Confident · modern · gala-formal
Best for
A grown-up, evening-led celebration
Open in full ↗
IV

Botanical Garden

A soft daylight garden wedding. Warm white with sage, dusty rose and terracotta; hand-drawn olive branches and eucalyptus stems; polaroid-feel photo frames; asymmetric, magazine-editorial layout. The friendliest of the four.

Type
DM Serif Display · Outfit · Caveat (sparingly)
Voice
Personal · warm · garden-party
Best for
A lived-in, less-formal evening
