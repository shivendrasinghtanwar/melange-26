The Three Milestones

One evening, three reasons to gather.

Three quiet stories, folded together into one warm Bikaner night.

I
The Reason

Love

A reception for Shivendra Singh Tanwar & Divyani Jain — two families, one improbable yes, and the easy beginning of a long and very ordinary life together. Come eat too much and stay too late.

II
The Occasion

Life

Sarandha Tanwar turns sixty — six decades of grace, mischief, and quiet courage worth toasting under a Bikaner sky. We're folding her birthday into the same evening, because of course we are.

III
The Farewell

Legacy

After a lifetime at Sophia School, she hangs up her chalk. Generations of students learned to think because of her — this evening is a small, glittering bow to all of that.
