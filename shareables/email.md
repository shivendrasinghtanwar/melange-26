# Email — invitation

Plain text. Modern mail clients (Apple Mail, Gmail's app, Outlook
365) render a rich preview of the URL using the same Open Graph
tags WhatsApp uses, so an attachment isn't necessary.

## Subject

```
Mélange — Tanwar family invitation, 30 June 2026
```

## Body

Replace `{{Name}}` before sending.

```
Dear {{Name}},

The Tanwar family would be honoured to have you with us in Bikaner on Tuesday, the 30th of June, 2026 — for one evening, with three reasons to gather:

  · the wedding reception of Shivendra Singh Tanwar & Divyani Jain
  · the 60th birthday of Sarandha Tanwar
  · her retirement from Sophia School, after a long teaching life

The full invitation — timeline, venue, and the RSVP card — is here:

  https://shivendrasinghtanwar.github.io/melange-26/

With love,
the Tanwar family
```
