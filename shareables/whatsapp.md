# WhatsApp — invitation messages

Two variants. Paste either into a WhatsApp chat after replacing
`{{Name}}` with the recipient's name. The URL inside auto-expands
into the rich preview card from `share-card.png`, so the long URL
itself almost disappears in the chat.

## Warm / personal

```
Hi {{Name}},

The Tanwar family would love to have you with us in Bikaner on the 30th of June, 2026 — for one evening, with three reasons to gather.

The full invitation, including the timeline, venue, and RSVP, is here:

https://shivendrasinghtanwar.github.io/melange-26/

With love,
the Tanwars
```

## Short / one-liner

When something briefer feels right — e.g., to acquaintances.

```
Mélange — 30 June 2026, Bikaner.
One evening, three reasons to gather.

https://shivendrasinghtanwar.github.io/melange-26/

— the Tanwar family
```
